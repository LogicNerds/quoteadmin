Quick Setup Guide

Welcome! Follow these simple steps to get your quote calculator up and running in minutes.

Step 1: Add Your Business Info

Fill in your company name, contact email, and phone number on this page.

Tip: Upload a logo to make your calculator look more professional!

Step 2: Add Your Products & Services

Go to "My Products & Services" and add the items you want to quote.

Tip: Choose "Fixed" for flat rates, "Per Unit" for quantity-based pricing, or "Custom" for jobs that vary.

Step 3: Customize Your Quote Page

Visit "My Quote Page" to customize the welcome message and colors.

Tip: Use the preview pane to see changes in real-time!

Step 4: Share Your Calculator

Copy the calculator link from "My Quote Page" and share it!

Tip: Add it to your website, social media bio, or email signature.

Step 5: Manage Your Quotes

Check "My Quotes" to see incoming quote requests and follow up with customers.

Tip: Use the status dropdown to track which quotes need follow-up!

That's it! You're ready to start collecting quotes. Questions? Feel free to explore the interface—everything is designed to be intuitive and easy to use.
